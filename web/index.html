OMORI for PSP

Turn your copy of the game into one EBOOT.PBP you can play on a PSP or in PPSSPP. It all runs here in your browser, and your game files never leave your computer.

You'll need the game itself, version 1.0.8 (it's on Steam), and either a PSP-2000, PSP-3000, PSP Go or PSP Street (E1000) with custom firmware, or the PPSSPP emulator. The PSP-1000 doesn't have enough memory, sadly. The EBOOT is big, so keep about 2 GB free on the memory stick. For building you need a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

1 Your game

Drop your game folder or a .zip here

It's the folder with OMORI.exe, Launch_OMORI.bat and www in it. On Steam, right-click the game, go to Manage and pick Browse local files.

It's saved in this browser, so next time you won't have to pick it again.

Controls: d-pad moves, cross confirms and talks to things, circle or start cancels and opens the menu, triangle tags (switches the party leader), hold square to run. L and R page through lists and do the map shortcuts (Q and W on PC), and select is the game's tab button. The analog stick isn't used.
